    Complete a task to earn an Arcade Token. Then use it to start a turn.

    How this connects.

    The arcade turn comes from your task list.

    Every completed task gives you one Arcade Token. A turn costs one token, so finishing tasks gives you the chance to play.

    1. Use the left and right controls to line up the claw.

    2. Press the drop button to lower the claw.

    3. Grab a prize to earn tickets, then return tomorrow to continue your streak.

    Tickets are your longer-term score.

    Ticket rewards

    Tickets stay saved on this device. You can use them to unlock small upgrades that make future turns more helpful.

    Win prizes to build enough tickets for an upgrade.
